# Acme CloudSync: Plans and Pricing

Prices are per user per month, excluding VAT.

## Starter

EUR 6 per user per month. Includes 100 GB of storage per user and 30 days of file version history.

## Business

EUR 12 per user per month. Includes 1 TB of storage per user, 180 days of version history, SSO and admin audit logs.

## Enterprise

Custom pricing for more than 250 users. Includes unlimited storage, a dedicated account manager and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Discounts

Customers who pay annually receive a 15% discount. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.
