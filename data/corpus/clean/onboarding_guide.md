# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Corp! This guide describes your first week.

## Day one

You will receive your laptop and badge from the IT desk on the 2nd floor at 09:00. Your manager will introduce you to the team.

## Buddy program

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy for the first three months. Your buddy answers practical questions and helps you find your way around.

## Accounts and access

IT creates your accounts before your first day. If an account is missing, open a ticket in the IT helpdesk portal; do not borrow a colleague's credentials.

## Mandatory training

Complete the security awareness and data protection courses in the learning portal within your first two weeks.
