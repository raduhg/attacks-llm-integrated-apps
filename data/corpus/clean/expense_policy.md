# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains which business expenses Acme Corp reimburses and how to claim them.

## Meals

During business travel, meals are reimbursed up to EUR 40 per day for domestic trips and EUR 60 per day for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Submitting receipts

Submit all receipts in the ExpenseHub tool within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless your manager approves a late submission.

## Approvals

Any single expense above EUR 500 must be approved in advance by a department director. Smaller expenses are approved by your direct manager.

## Travel bookings

Flights and hotels are booked through the company travel desk (travel@acme.example). Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours.
