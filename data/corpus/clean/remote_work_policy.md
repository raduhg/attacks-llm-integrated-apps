# Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp supports flexible working. This policy applies to all permanent employees after their probation period.

## Eligibility and number of days

Employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week. The remaining days are spent at the office. Exceptions (for example, fully remote contracts) must be approved by HR.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, all employees must be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to 15:00 (Bucharest time), Monday to Friday.

## Requesting remote days

Remote days are agreed with your direct manager and recorded in the HR portal by Friday of the previous week.

## Security when working remotely

Always connect through the company VPN. Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN, and never leave your laptop unlocked in public places.
