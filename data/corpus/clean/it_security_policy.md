# IT Security Policy

All employees are responsible for protecting Acme Corp's systems and data.

## Passwords and MFA

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all customer-facing systems. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

## Reporting phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links. Forward it to security@acme.example and delete it from your inbox. The security team replies within one business day.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and receive updates automatically. Personal USB drives must not be connected to company laptops.

## Customer data

Customer personal data may only be accessed when needed for a support or billing task, and must never be sent to personal or external addresses.
