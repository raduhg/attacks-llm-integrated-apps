# Code of Conduct

Acme Corp expects every employee to act with integrity and respect.

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from business partners only if their value is below EUR 50. Gifts above this value must be declined or reported to the compliance team.

## Confidentiality

Internal documents, customer data and unreleased product plans are confidential. Do not discuss them outside the company or post them on social media.

## Reporting concerns

Concerns can be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline. Acme Corp does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
