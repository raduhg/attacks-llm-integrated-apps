# Acme CloudSync: Setup Guide

Acme CloudSync is our file synchronisation product for businesses. This guide is used by the support team.

## Installing the desktop client

Download the desktop client from the customer portal. It runs on Windows 10 or later, macOS 13 or later, and Ubuntu 22.04 or later.

## Signing in

Customers sign in with their company single sign-on (SSO). Starter plan customers without SSO sign in with email and password plus MFA.

## Sync folder

By default the client creates a folder named "CloudSync" in the user's home directory. Users can change the location in Settings > Sync.

## Mobile apps

Mobile apps for iOS and Android support viewing and uploading files, but not selective sync.
